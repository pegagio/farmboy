# Prepare specification contract — approved revised boundary

`$farmboy-prepare-specification` retains its named/next-eligible entry point and one-session/current-model baseline. It prepares a saved draft using existing project templates/setup and roadmap/wiki/brief capabilities, then ends automation.

## Automated outcomes

- **Draft ready for interactive clarification:** Usable saved draft, explicit unresolved topics and actual quality/fit findings. Successful preparation ends here; existing ordinary `$speckit-clarify` is the next operator-started step.
- **Draft ready for human specification review:** No material ambiguity; saved artifacts and review handoff. Do not invent questions.
- **Blocked/incomplete:** Unresolved identity/governance, missing capability or unusable draft. A paused/stalled/budget-limited Goal is not successful handoff evidence.

For both successful outcomes, the native Goal’s objective is saved draft plus handoff, not answered questions or approval. Confirm actual native completion; prose is insufficient. The Goal must not remain active awaiting human answers. This contract does not claim an agent-controlled Goal pause API.

## Interactive continuation and approval

After handoff, invoke existing clarify ordinarily outside the completed preparation Goal. Preserve actual input waits, supported rounds/limits and current-artifact reconciliation; no replacement Farmboy clarification skill. Completion of clarification leads to explicit human spec review. Human approval is prerequisite for planning, regardless of review depth. No planning, tasks, implementation, convergence, closeout, integration or push follows by inference.

## State and ownership

Use current roadmap/pointer/spec identity and existing metadata/notes, not a new ledger or schema. Distinguish interrupted drafting from completed handoff, pending interactive input and pending human review. Do not reactivate a completed Goal merely to restore a pending question. Current files remain mutable before integration merge. Handoff and native completion are not approval, historical freeze or permission to transfer a still-owned Feature/workspace without its governing rules.
