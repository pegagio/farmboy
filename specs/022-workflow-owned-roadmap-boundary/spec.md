# Workflow-Owned Roadmap Boundary

The operator approved this boundary and explicitly requested its implementation on `codex/roadmap-boundary-correction` on 2026-10-10. This follow-forward feature corrects the lifecycle ownership introduced by Feature 020 without editing that merged history. FarmBoy Feature 021 remains paused at its checkpoint; FlowKit names are preserved. The active pointer and roadmap are unchanged by this implementation.

## Approved Scope

FlowKit owns milestone decisions, current artifacts and dependencies, native reviewer provenance, authoring-ready evidence, source/route compatibility, adjacent-edge preparation and revalidation, no-op/retry decisions, independent readback and actual effect records. The independent roadmap extension owns configured path validation, ledger structure/identity, generic explicitly authorized amendments, standard version/provenance updates and unrelated-entry preservation. Use the existing controller and generic roadmap-write interface; introduce no adapter framework or configuration system.

Specify may advance planned to specced after authoring and brief checks. Implement may advance specced to in-progress after readiness checks and before coding. Close Out may advance in-progress to implemented after completion and independent review checks. Preserve the separate verified and acceptance decisions. Failed, stale, ambiguous or partial results prevent further work. Consistent advanced states produce no duplicate transition or downgrade. Recovery remains bounded to individually substantiated adjacent edges within the invoking phase ceiling.

## Required Behavior

- **Workflow ownership**: The controller must evaluate lifecycle and authoring proof from its own delivered source, without importing an extension lifecycle module or requiring the extension to recognize FlowKit identities.
- **Explicit amendment**: Every ready revalidation must return the complete selected entry/status delta, preimage digest, authority reference and current evidence paths through ordinary roadmap-write input. Evidence cannot establish trusted invocation authority.
- **Independent readback**: A separate named Reviewer must compare actual ledger bytes and current source/evidence against the retained proposal. Only the selected heading and standard amendment metadata may change. Record applied, not-applied or indeterminate effects independently of command success.
- **Partial effects**: An applied status remains applied after a failed command, changed source or receipt persistence failure. Block and preserve the effect; reassess before retry. Retain original occurrence evidence without reconstruction or backdating.
- **Authoring boundary**: Validate exact authoring/checklist/Reviewer proof in FlowKit before the generic brief. Do not require implementation, Complete status or a future brief to establish authored readiness.
- **Separate cleanup**: Record the extension cleanup contract in this feature, but do not modify that repository, its existing uncommitted work or command/extension identities here.

## Validation and Exclusions

Disposable generic consumers must cover all three phase graphs, source/evidence gates, no-op and deferred states, repeat readback, failed commands, partial/unrelated edits and receipt-recording failure. Existing workflow graph/gate, controller, catalog and lifecycle regressions remain applicable. Joint fresh-consumer and live-agent acceptance require the separately corrected roadmap snapshot and are not established by a generic fixture.

Renaming, publication, merging, commits, roadmap verification, feature acceptance, repository consolidation, installed-consumer refresh and new dependencies are outside this request. The separate integration-None CLI dispatch failure remains independently tracked.
