# Delivery native handoff

A genuine required-input boundary saves INCOMPLETE delivery, exact question, current artifacts, actual work/remaining issues and ordinary next action in existing notes. A separately selected matching bounded Goal may complete only after durable save/readback, supported completion and native complete readback before asking or waiting. This completes the handoff objective, not delivery or approval. (S009)

An unmet delivery-only objective, unknown helper failure or unavailable control cannot become successful input handoff. Native host-state mutation or invented receipts are forbidden. After an actual answer, separately selected ordinary interaction continues outside the completed Goal; automatic Goal restart/create is not authorized. (S009)

Resume inspects current artifacts, Git/roadmap and approval applicability, preserves answers/task identities/evidence, refreshes affected checks and avoids duplicate effects for a valid pending review. Actual workflow verification requires a separate executing subject and evaluator; fixture answers cannot accept real Farmboy work, and bootstrap limits cannot be filled with future or recursively manufactured evidence. (S009)
