# Abandoned workflow agent capacity proposal

Feature 018 was abandoned on 2026-10-03 because the operator rejected its approach and specification. Its requirements, plan, tasks, and validation artifacts remain an investigation record, not active implementation scope. The capacity observation remains unresolved; the disposition selects no replacement approach or feature. (S024)

## Historical proposal

The proposal sought sustained sequential workflow use in one persistent Codex chat, preservation of results before releasing eligible completed children, bounded exhaustion recovery, and installation/upgrade guidance for consumer-owned configuration. It proposed retaining children needed for answers or continuation and preserving exact named dispatch. These are abandoned requirements, not established controller behavior. (S024)

## Evidence boundary

The specification records a successful Specifier readiness probe followed by a Reviewer launch failure before Clarify work. The reported twelve-child count does not establish the operative limit or root cause. Earlier reversible archival that restored a launch is historical evidence, not proof of the current cause or a selected production mechanism. A capacity increase alone was not sufficient proof of resolution under the proposed validation contract. (S024)

## Roadmap disposition

The roadmap retains Feature 018 as abandoned with its exact specification-directory mapping and historical investigation questions. It selects no capacity value or replacement approach and leaves Features 016 and 017 unchanged. (S018)
