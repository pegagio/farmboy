# Approved local delivery

The selected delivery composition requires matching explicit/active identity, contained artifacts, verified dependencies and applicable explicit specification/full-plan approval. It prescribes actual installed implementation, verification, convergence and separate in-scope repair with current-artifact flow-back; standalone implementation does not acquire that authority. These are workflow prescriptions, not proof of a particular delivery. (S009)

Closeout prescribes delivery documentation, attributable roadmap debrief, one-source wiki ingestion and lint. Its own narrow authorization permits only implemented; debrief recommendations authorize no status, scope, dependency or governance amendment. All scoped artifacts and implemented belong in the final local commit, preserving unrelated work. Success is reported after actual commit success; ordinary Git errors receive honest reporting without special recovery machinery. (S009)

Successful delivery ends Awaiting result review. Only later explicit applicable human result acceptance separately authorizes verified; integration, merge, push, completion declarations and future004 bridge work remain separate. No new controller, persistence, ledger, counter/reset or scoring machinery is prescribed. (S009)

Feature003’s explicitly approved bootstrap sequence keeps precommit checks and independent real-Farmboy execution required, then independently observes the actual final Farmboy commit/result after it exists and before successful human result-review handoff. Pending observation does not block its own commit and is never passed in advance; its actual result is reported in ordinary conversation without a receipt-only commit or dirty tail. This sequencing approval is separate from human result acceptance. (S010)

[Native handoff and ordinary continuation](./delivery-native-handoff.md) describes the distinct bounded automation endpoint. (S009)
