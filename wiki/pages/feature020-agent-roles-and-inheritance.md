# Feature 020 named agents for delegated steps

## Feature 020 supplemental readiness role

Feature 020 adds exact `FlowKit-Readiness Checker` to the original eight prefixed roles, making a nine-name inventory. It checks preparation evidence and reports readiness gaps without editing artifacts, replacing Reviewer, granting authority or claiming acceptance. Exact launch evidence for this supplemental role is required alongside the original role matrix. Earlier Feature 015/017 eight-role histories and their effective-settings limitations remain intact. (S027)

One-time supported preparation targets future chats in the checkout and prepared Codex-managed worktrees while preserving consumer-owned configuration. Definition delivery, exact native availability, UI labels, effective model/effort, client version and first-chat startup evidence are distinct. Historical failure does not prove a current failure or startup cause; preparation promises no current-chat reload, universal client behavior, capacity remedy or per-chat installation. [Preparation gates](./preimplementation-review-gates.md) links the supplemental role to current independent evidence. (S027)
