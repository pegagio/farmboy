# FlowKit agent configuration and consumer delivery

Feature 017 defines a prospective agent contract. Its clarification decisions establish consumer-owned delivery and role-based model selection; they do not prove implementation, release, adoption, or acceptance. Existing installation guidance remains baseline provenance. (S025)

## Identities and responsibilities

The eight intended exact names are FlowKit-Roadmap Agent, FlowKit-Specifier, FlowKit-Planner, FlowKit-Tasker, FlowKit-Reviewer, FlowKit-Coder, FlowKit-Code Reviewer, and FlowKit-Wiki Curator. Current delegated references on every branch, discovery, filenames, validation, and documentation must agree without compatibility aliases or responsibility changes. Historical verified artifacts retain their meaning. (S025)

Role reviews cover allowed work, outputs and mutations, exclusions, questions, blockers, and evidence. Authors retain self-checks; independent reviewers must not review their own work. Main-task questions and consequential gates stay with the parent. Missing or unavailable assignments stop without fallback. (S025)

Planning researches supported model/effort defaults separately by responsibility and presents all eight choices, suitability rationale, and client-support evidence for operator approval before implementation. Exact values are not selected by clarification. Workflow model maps, assignment defaults, and per-run agent/model/effort overrides remain excluded. (S025)

## Consumer-owned copies and explicit resets

The selected catalog delivery contract adds missing default configurations while preserving existing files. Consumers own the copies and may customize instructions or settings. Ordinary refresh updates available defaults without overwriting those copies. This prospectively replaces the earlier exclusion of agent acquisition from catalog delivery; it does not establish current installer behavior. (S025)

Explicit reset-one or reset-all reapplies the selected package's defaults and discards customizations only in targeted current FlowKit agents. Unrelated agents survive. A prefix or matching filename grants no overwrite authority outside an explicitly requested reset. Concrete operation syntax and reusable source packaging remain planning matters. (S025)

Removal preserves consumer copies. Renamed or retired definitions are documented; old files remain until operator removal. Per-file ownership hashes, automatic customization detection, automatic merging, and managed ownership of agent copies are excluded. Lifecycle reports distinguish completed changes from failures and give bounded recovery for partial operations. (S025)

## Validation and scope

Validation covers all eight names, active delegated references, and disposable-consumer install, refresh, reset, migration, collision, partial-operation, and removal cases. Portable records identify components, versions, source coordinates/digests, tested client/CLI, inputs, and observations without raw transcripts, secrets, or absolute host paths. (S025)

Configuration consistency, supported settings, live exact dispatch, and observed effective model/effort are separate evidence categories. Labels, static checks, and no-op dispatch do not establish live behavior; unavailable effective-setting evidence remains unverified. Local success implies neither publication nor acceptance. (S025)

Features 011, 014, and 015 are prerequisites. Feature 016 remains independent: coordination covers names and validation only, without changing probe timing or unrelated control flow. The generic bundle/component boundary and The Diagram's separate consumer/adapter authority remain intact. (S025)
