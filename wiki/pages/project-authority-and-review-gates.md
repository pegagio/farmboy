# Project authority and review gates

## Constitutional FarmBoy identity

Constitution 8.0.0, amended on 2026-10-10, makes FarmBoy and “Spec et Labora” the product identity, `farmboy` the project/bundle ID, `farmboy-<purpose>` the owned skills/workflows and exact `FarmBoy <role>` the project-owned agents. Owned feedback IDs and delegation metadata use FarmBoy identities; independent roadmap/wiki extension IDs and core Spec Kit commands retain their identities. A bundle composes independently versioned components rather than owning their behavior. (S035)

The repository owns generic workflow source, separately approved presets and portable feedback; maintainer intake stays separate from generic delivery. The Diagram remains a consumer, with its adapter under separate authority. Reviewed source packages, rather than installed consumer copies, are the change authority. (S035)

## Human decisions and bounded transition

The operator selects workflow and scope. Invocation authorizes reviewed named assignments; missing, invalid or unavailable assignments stop without fallback. Declared bounded correction may run within that workflow, while consequential questions remain in the main task and continuation returns to the same child. Completion grants no later workflow, roadmap verification, Git integration or acceptance. Direct trusted bounded roadmap instructions authorize derived wording after target, dependency and governing-state validation; unsettled scope, constitutional, authority and product decisions require human gates. (S035)

The identity change is a pure cutover with no old invocation aliases, legacy-key fallback or migration feature. Previously reviewed source and installed components retain their identities until separately invoked rename implementation and compatible installation take effect. Thus an old installed FlowKit controller remains operative during this bounded transition; the amendment itself grants neither implementation nor acceptance, publication or Git authority. Historical records and actual repository URLs retain their meaning. (S035)

[Earlier authority contracts](./project-authority-history.md) preserve the dated S001/S017 accounts and Feature 014/020 decision evolution. Constitution 8 preserves human authority, component boundaries, workflow purposes, gates, role responsibilities and model/effort settings while changing owned identity. No-op dispatch remains distinct from live-agent evidence; local tests prove no publication, stock compatibility or adoption. (S035)
